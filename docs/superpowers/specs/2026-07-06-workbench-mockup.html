Composer · workbench concept

Inbox → triage → pipeline: one workbench, many sources

Anything you flag — a Readwise highlight, a Bluesky post, an email, a stray note — lands in the Inbox and clusters around its referent (the underlying thing). You triage each capture — by hand or an AI chat — into results: to-dos, questions, and connections. The action-results then flow onto a Pipeline you work with AI. Add sources to watch clusters assemble; step the stage to walk the flow.
